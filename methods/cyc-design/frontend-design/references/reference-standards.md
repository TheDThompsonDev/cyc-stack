# Reference standards

Four sites held up as the bar, inspected on 2026-11-02 in a desktop browser with computed styles read from the page where possible. Each entry records what was observed and the rule to transfer. Transfer the mechanism, not the colors; copying a palette produces a costume, not a design.

## Undersold.ai (resume and career product)

Observed: canvas `#f8f5ee`, ink `#171b18`, brand green `#104034`, gold accent `#e5a128` and `#805000`, mint `#9ce4c6` for small marks. Display in DM Serif Display at 50 px with tracking −1.75 px and line height 1.03; body in DM Sans 16 px. One handwritten phrase per heading ("prove it.") in Kalam, gold, with a hand-drawn underline. Tracked uppercase eyebrow in gold above the headline ("FREE SCORE + BASELINE + GUIDED INTERVIEW + REBUILD"). Full-bleed photograph of a person at a laptop, scrim only over the text half. Primary action in brand green with an arrow; secondary as an outlined "See how it works" with a play icon; a three-item trust row beneath (every bullet scored, baseline yours to keep, no credit card). An open-beta notice in plain words. Dark green band next with a product screenshot in a browser frame. Pricing as three cards where the featured plan is the dark card with a gold header; prices as oversized serif numerals.

Transfer: the opening answers "what is this and what do I get" in eyebrow, headline, two sentences and a trust row, with a photograph that has a subject. One accent voice per heading. The dark section is the product demonstration. The featured plan is the one that looks different.

## Rep & Plate (daily health tool, product name Fuel)

Observed: sidebar workspace (archetype 6). Canvas `#f8faf7` to `#fbfdfb`, brand `#17674e`, ink `#102333`, sage tints for surfaces. Display in Lora with the second phrase in italic muted green ("Your day, *taken care of.*"); body in DM Sans. Eyebrow date in tracked caps. First row: a nutrition card with a ring chart and three macro bars beside a dark photographic workout card with its own action. Second row: three stat cards that differ in content (steps with a sentence, sleep as 7h 12m with mixed numeral sizes, a "2 little things to check" note on a tinted surface). Records listed with small food photographs and "Estimated · editable" under each. Status pill "LOCAL DEMO" in the header. Phone: five-item bottom bar, chat as the primary surface, suggestion chips, "Just on this device" line under the input.

Transfer: cards differ by content shape, not by icon. Real-looking records with honest labels beat invented stats. The warm voice lives in the display line and the one-sentence notes, not in every label. Demo state is declared where a visitor could mistake it for a save.

## Atlassian.com (software marketing)

Observed: one blue (`#0052cc` family) on white, a geometric sans at one weight for headings, a short headline ("Discover the power of knowing"), a pill primary button and a text link with an arrow as the secondary. The hero's second half is a dense, real product screenshot, not an illustration. A logo strip with gray logos. A dark section ("Connect the work behind the work") with a node-graph illustration on the right and the copy on the left. Sections change surface (white, dark, light tint) and density, not just background stripe.

Transfer: restraint is a decision; one color used with authority reads as a system. Show the real product at readable size. Change density and surface between sections.

## AHS.com (American Home Shield, home warranty)

Observed: utility bar, then header with an outlined "Log in" and a filled "Shop Plans" pill. A red promotional band with an icon and one sentence. The hero is a white rounded card on pale blue holding a serif display in red and navy ("Fall is here. Savings are too. 50% off select plans"), two sentences, and a zip-code input joined to a "Shop Plans" button (archetype 4). An awards strip of badges beneath. Plan comparison as three cards where the middle one carries a "Most Popular" header and a highlighted border; each lists specific limits ($50,000 total limit per agreement term, $5,000 per covered HVAC system). Curved section dividers. A photograph of a technician in a branded jacket at a red door.

Transfer: when the visitor came to buy or request, the input is the hero. Plans are compared with real numbers. Proof is specific (limits, awards, a technician at a real door), not "trusted by thousands".
