# Teach

Use for learning rather than merely receiving a completed artifact. Stages: [frame](../../../../methods/cyc-frame/SKILL.md) → [understand](../../../../methods/cyc-understand/SKILL.md) → [teach](../../../../methods/cyc-teach/SKILL.md) → [deliver](../../../../methods/cyc-deliver/SKILL.md).

Establish what the learner wants to understand and whether they want explanation, guided practice, or explanation alongside implementation. Inspect their real project before teaching from it.

Use [Understand](../../../../methods/cyc-understand/SKILL.md) to establish accurate behavior and [Teach](../../../../methods/cyc-teach/SKILL.md) to build a mental model with a concrete example. Show relevant decisions and tradeoffs without attributing inferred rationale to an author. Offer practice only when useful or requested, and adapt feedback to the learner's actual response. Existing authorization to build can coexist with explanations; a teaching-only request does not authorize modifying the application.

When confusion persists, change the example or expose an overlooked prerequisite. Preserve the distinction between what was explained and what was assessed.

**Acceptance:** for an explanation, accurate, accessible material addressing the question. For demonstrated learning, actual learner work. Waiting for that work is a valid blocked state; never invent mastery or complete the exercise merely to advance the chain.
