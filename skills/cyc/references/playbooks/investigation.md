# Investigation

Use to explain how or why something behaves as it does, without changing it. Stages: [scout](../../../../methods/cyc-scout/SKILL.md) → [deliver](../../../../methods/cyc-deliver/SKILL.md).

In the scout pass, search for the entry point and trace the path through the code, reading only what the question needs, and run the code when reading is ambiguous. Deliver the answer as a short chain of file:line hops, with observed facts separated from inferences. For deeper questions, [Understand](../../../../methods/cyc-understand/SKILL.md) has the full procedure.

**Acceptance:** the question is answered with cited file:line evidence, inferences are labeled, and no product files changed. What could not be established is stated.
